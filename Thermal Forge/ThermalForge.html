Airflow 101

Front & bottom fans pull cool air in; rear & top fans push hot air out. Keep slightly more intake than exhaust to reduce dust. Mesh fronts breathe best.

Why GPU models differ

The same chip comes in Premium big 3–4 fan coolers (ROG Astral/Strix, Suprim, AORUS Master, Nitro+), Mid (TUF, Gaming Trio, Gaming OC, Pulse) and Budget (Dual, Ventus, Windforce) versions. That can mean a 10–15°C difference.
